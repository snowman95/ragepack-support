About the game

RAGEPACK is an inventory deck-building game by Auto. Arrange weapons and armor in your pack and connect their star effects to build your barbarian. Explore four regions with three heroes, unlock equipment through victories, and take on hardcore expeditions. The game interface is currently Korean.

Playing and saving

Play in landscape. Tap Start Expedition, then choose a hero. In the pack, tap an item and its destination, or drag it into place. Battles run automatically and can be switched to manual controls.

Expedition progress and unlocks are saved on your device. If you close the app during a battle, it resumes from the start of that battle. Deleting the app can remove progress; a copy may remain depending on your device backup settings.
